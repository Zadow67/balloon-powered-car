# Balloon Powered Car

A simple 3D printable car powered by the air released from a balloon.

## How it works

The car uses the air escaping from a balloon to create thrust. Attach a balloon to the air tube, inflate it, place the car on a smooth surface, and release the air.

## Features

* Balloon-powered movement
* 3D printable parts
* Designed in Onshape
* Simple assembly
* Demonstrates basic thrust and air pressure

## Files

`models/` contains the printable STL and 3MF files.

`source/` contains the original CAD file.

`images/` contains photos and renders of the car.

## How to use

1. Print the required parts.
2. Assemble the wheels, axles and car body.
3. Attach a balloon to the air tube.
4. Inflate the balloon.
5. Place the car on a smooth, flat surface.
6. Release the balloon and watch it move.

## Design

The car was designed in Onshape.
